# Session 1
* Party tracked down and killed Sephek Caltros, and can now collect the bounty on his head if they get back to EastHaven and claim their reward.  They have his frozen head in tow. 
* The party investigated the "Lake Monster"
  * The Laker Monster was actually a Plesiosaurus, which the Druid knew was normally docile and fearful of men.  It normally eats fish and plants.
  * The Plesiosaurus had been awakened by a Druid named Ravisin, who had told it to plague the people of Bremen, or he would take its awakened gift away.
  * The party persuaded it this was wrong, and got it to stop attacking fisherman.  Thus resolving the Lake Monster issue, and gaining fame.  
  * The party was awarded 5gp and a scroll of animal freindship from a contact in town. 3. 
* The party determined to collect their reward, heading to EastHaven, and then head south to Good Mead to resolve the issue with a Giant Stealing the Mead supply there.  All of 10 towns is out of or nearly out of drinking mead until this is resolved.  The Mead must flow!
* Thus, the party is heading out of Bremen, to Bryn Shander first.

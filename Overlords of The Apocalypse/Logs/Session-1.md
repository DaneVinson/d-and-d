# Session 1
The story begins in New Womford on the day of the Greengrass festival.  Strings, Tahcryn, Luskar and Pyle arrive to find the town abuzz with the news of an assassination attempt on Mayor Glim.  The party met up at the Church of Torm to interrogate one of the assassins who quickly divulged that he's working for the Zhentarim and that his main contact was Nalasker at the Bargewright Inn.

Whipped up into a proper Lather of Justice, Preston and the gang set off at once to the Bargewright Inn to dispense some stern legal advice.  Nalasker knew he was in trouble the second the party...  barged in.  His gang of local flunkies was no match for the party.  Pyle suddenly developed a strange fixation for "table surfing," which continued for most of the session.  After taking Nalasker into custody, the Inn's cook confessed that he saw some "shady characters" coming in and out through the cellar.  A quick investigation revealed a rough hewn passage under the inn.

Traveling several miles down the claustrophobic passage, the party eventually came to an assassin's hideout: the Thieves' Highway.  After dealing with some minor traps and other inconveniences (though Glim nearly did die when he was exposed to a Cloudkill trap), the party had their first real test against a Master Assassin and his skilled cronies.  The DM rolled many crits; there was much poison damage.  If Pyle didn't have poison resistance, he would've died twice over from the damage he sustained.  Strings nearly expired when the Master Assassin snuck up on him.  After a tough fight, the Master Assassin was forced to flee.  The party found some strange articles in a hidden shrine connected to his bedchamber: a cruel looking dagger and some kind of dreadful book.

After a couple of hours the party methodically cleared out the rest of the hideout, rescuing a kidnapped official from New Womford and finding the corpse of a knight who ran afoul of the Zhentarim, along with a bit of gold, a Radiant Torch, a Smokepowder Cask and a dog-eared copy of some lascivious gnomish pornography.  On the other side of the hideout the party found another earthen passage continuing on, but chose to return to town to deliver the freed prisoner and to fully rest up.  This "Belexas" individual, referenced in certain pieces of evidence as being in charge of the Zhentarim operation, no doubt lurks somewhere deeper in the complex.

# Session 2
After clearing out the first portion of the Thieves’ Highway, the party returned to Womford with some rescued prisoners and rested for one night.  Preston purged the unholy text Litany of the Flayed in Torm’s good name.

Once everyone was fit, the party returned to the Zhentarim lair to track down the mysterious “Belexas” and to learn more about the attempt on Glim’s life.  Preston called upon his influence with the Order of the Gauntlet to bring a squad of knights with him so that they could hold & protect the portion of the hideout that the party had already cleared.

After traveling a considerable number of miles underground, the party arrived at a second hideout complex.  Gaining access was easy enough, but once inside the party was faced with a multi-layered Glyph trap that caused considerable damage before expiring.  After these expensive countermeasures were exhausted, the party pushed deeper into the complex and very quickly came face to face with the local Zhentarim Underboss, Belexas the Beholder, and his assassin henchmen.

The ensuing battle was pretty furious.  Glim was downed when he chose an ill-favored hiding spot near a nest of Bodaks.  The addition of the Bodaks put the outcome of the battle very much into high risk territory, but the party managed to prevail after burning a considerable amount of resources.

A short rest in Belexas’ war room was only briefly inconvenienced by an undead patrol.  Inside the party found several secret nooks containing a large quantity of loot: a bunch of coins, some secret Zhentarim files, including the signed contract on Glim’s life, and some rare magic items -- a curious flask of smuggling and the Hatecraft Signet, a powerful legacy magic item.

With the bulk of the Hideout’s forces already dealt with, the party had an easy time mopping up the rest of the place.  A poncy wizard pretentiously referring to himself as “The Administrator” was captured.  He claims that he’s “just a contractor” and not really affiliated with the Black Network, but evidence found in his quarters suggested that he is responsible for torturing and abusing the Harper prisoners that were later found in the compound (Janessa Sparrow and Silas Thorne).  

The party also found a subterranean lake with a couple of strange, covered skiffs beached nearby.  A mask-and-bottled-air apparatus discovered in another room appears to be connected with these odd boats in some way.

A Priest of Bane who was conducting some kind of evil necromantic surgery on Silas Thorne was slain by Pyle.  With his dying breath, he issued an ominous curse upon his killer.  I guess we’ll see what that’s all about later…

The party earned 69,040xp (11.506xp each).

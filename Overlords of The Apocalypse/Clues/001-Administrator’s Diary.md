# Administrator’s Diary

**Janessa... Oh Janessa, how you haunt my dreams.**

I think she really could learn to love me, given some time. Perhaps if we break her mind, we could relieve her of her allegiance to the foul Harpers and persuade her to see things our way? Then she and I could really be together at last. *Heartbound.*

Or, if that proves impossible, I could always turn her over to the Priest and have him turn her into one of his soulless automatons. While I would prefer Janessa to be whole, if I must choose between her body and her mind? Well, I can do all the thinking for the both of us.

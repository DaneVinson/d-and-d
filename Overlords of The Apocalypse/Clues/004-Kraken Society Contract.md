# Kraken Society Contract

**Memorandum of Termination Services:**  
Executed this day between the Zhentarim, hereafter *"The Network,"* and the Kraken Society, hereafter *"The Client."*

The Network agrees to provide permanent cessation of vital functions for one Glim Knacker, Mayor of New Womford, by whichever methods The Network deems appropriate. Full payment to be discharged upon receipt of proof of death delivery to The Flying Snake, Waterdeep.

**Signed:** Belexas, Underboss, Dessarin Valley Chapter  
**Counter-Signed:** Semmonemily, Client

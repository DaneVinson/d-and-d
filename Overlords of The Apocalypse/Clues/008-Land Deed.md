# Land Deed

The bearer of this document is herefore granted full title to The Wink & Kiss, along with its inventory and incomes, in perpetuity or until such time that the bearer transmits this document to a different owner. All privileges and rights pertaining to said property shall be granted to the bearer forthwith.

_If this is a forgery, it is excellently done, but as far as you can tell, those seals are legitimate. This document makes you the legal owner of The Wink & Kiss, which to the best of your recollections is a tavern in Yartar that was once owned by the Hands of Yartar thieves’ guild._

# The Poisoner’s Plot

...Our plan is finally entering the final stage. After months of effort, our agents in Yartar have gained access to the Sauceworks. Grimbax has made good on his delivery of the toxin — soon we shall know if the fortune we spent on *“Simmaker’s Surprise”* is indeed worth it. Personally, I never want to deal with the Yugoloths again. Deceit is all they understand and, if Liches could fart, Gehenna is what that would smell like. If it all comes off as we hope, we should recoup our investment fivefold once the Waterbaron pays our ransom demands.
